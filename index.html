The 5 Stages of Food Safety Inspection

Each stage is rigorously audited out of 20 points (Total: 100 Points)

Stage 1 • Max 20 Pts

Food Quality & Storage

Fresh raw ingredients, temperature-controlled refrigeration (below 4°C), safe hot holding (above 63°C), and strict First-In First-Out (FIFO) stock rotation.

Stage 2 • Max 20 Pts

Kitchen Cleanliness & Hygiene

Food-grade stainless steel prep surfaces, commercial dishwashing sterilization, sanitized cutting boards, exhaust grease traps, and locked chemical storage.

Stage 3 • Max 20 Pts

Dining Ambiance & Environment

Customer dining area cleanliness, cross-ventilation and air quality, adequate natural/ambient lighting, sanitized restroom amenities, and non-slip flooring.

Stage 4 • Max 20 Pts

Staff Health & Personal Hygiene

Clean chef uniforms, mandatory hairnets and disposable gloves, hands-free pedal washbasins with antibacterial soap, and verified annual medical fitness certificates.

Stage 5 • Max 20 Pts

Water Quality & Waste Disposal

Certified potable Reverse Osmosis (RO) drinking water, color-coded covered pedal garbage bins, regular grease-trap drainage, and sealed pest barriers.

How FoodGuard Works

A streamlined workflow from on-site audit to public transparency

1

On-Site Audit

An authorized Food Safety Officer inspects kitchen, dining, and storage facilities across 20 parameters.

2

Scoring & Grading

The system computes percentage points out of 100 and automatically assigns a certified letter grade (A to F).

3

Live Publishing

The officer publishes the audit report, saving the official certificate directly into the public safety database.

4

Public Search

Citizens and diners search by restaurant name to review complete inspection certificates before dining.

Restaurant & Audit Details

Stage 1: Food Quality & Storage

Score: 20/20 | Grade: A

1.1 Raw Ingredients & Freshness

Fresh vegetables and verified raw meat suppliers.

1.2 Cold Storage & Temperature Control

Refrigeration below 4°C; deep freezer below -18°C.

1.3 Cooking Temperature & Cross-Contamination

Adequate boiling/frying; separate veg & non-veg prep.

1.4 Food Rotation (FIFO) & Expiry Checking

Clear expiration dates; First-In First-Out compliance.

Stage 2: Kitchen Cleanliness & Hygiene

Score: 20/20 | Grade: A

2.1 Food Preparation Surfaces & Countertops

Stainless steel countertops, disinfected between preps.

2.2 Cookware, Utensils & Dishwashing

Commercial high-temp washing; clean storage racks.

2.3 Kitchen Floors, Drains & Exhaust Vents

Non-greasy floor tiles; degreased chimney filters.

2.4 Chemical & Cleaning Supplies Storage

Detergents stored away from food prep areas.

Stage 3: Dining Ambiance & Environment

Score: 20/20 | Grade: A

3.1 Seating Area & Dining Table Hygiene

Sanitized dining tables; stain-free cutlery & napkins.

3.2 Ventilation, Air Quality & Odor Control

Fresh air circulation; absence of smoke or damp smell.

3.3 Lighting & Physical Space Safety

Well-lit dining halls; unblocked emergency exits.

3.4 Restroom Cleanliness & Handwash Supply

Regularly disinfected restrooms with soap and dryers.

Stage 4: Staff Health & Personal Hygiene

Score: 20/20 | Grade: A

4.1 Uniforms, Aprons & Hair Restraints

Clean coats, aprons, caps/hairnets; trimmed nails.

4.2 Handwashing Compliance & Gloves

Staff wash hands frequently; disposable gloves for plating.

4.3 Staff Medical Fitness & Sick Leave Policy

Annual health fitness certificates; no sick staff on duty.

4.4 Food Safety Training & Certifications

Staff trained in safe food handling and hygiene protocols.

Stage 5: Water Quality & Waste Management

Score: 20/20 | Grade: A

5.1 Drinking Water & Ice Machine Safety

RO filtered water certified; sanitized ice dispensers.

5.2 Solid Waste Disposal & Trash Bins

Color-coded, foot-pedal covered bins with garbage liners.

5.3 Liquid Waste, Grease Traps & Drainage

Free-flowing floor drains; weekly grease-trap clearance.

5.4 Pest Control & Physical Prevention

Fly-catchers active; monthly professional pest audit log.
